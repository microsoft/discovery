# Review a research source

Identify the source supplied by the user.
Confirm that its contents can be read before summarizing it.
Report its research question, method, main findings, and limitations.
Identify the passages supporting each factual claim.
If source contents aren't available, explain what's missing.
Don't infer results from a filename or title.
