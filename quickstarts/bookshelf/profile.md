# Bookshelf runner profile — public SCLC variant

This profile and [prompt](prompts/hero.txt) are proposed. Use
[the guide](README.md) and stop until its owner, model/index recipe and answer
checks are agreed. Never borrow approval or validation from Rebecca's internal
control, CMS's skincare corpus or SiPhy's engineering papers.

## Planned sequence, not executed proof

1. Inspect capabilities and all manifest identities. Report unresolved settings
   and public-asset readiness. Do not install/index/invoke a model at intake.
2. With explicit acquisition approval, obtain the exact fifteen permitted
   PDF versions and verify sizes/hashes through the shared helper. Keep licence
   notices and record original URLs. No substitutions on download failure.
3. Configure the approved local agent/Bookshelf route after separate permission.
   Creating a shelf is not indexing; indexing is not usable content. Record
   provider/embedding/chunk settings and document/chunk identities.
4. Inspect extracted text and actual query passages from the approved index;
   diagnose protection notices, missing/duplicate content and empty retrieval.
   Diagnostic word matches alone do not prove citation correctness.
5. After readiness and separate agent-computation approval, dispatch
   `bookshelf-researcher` with the exact accepted prompt and only the fixed
   public SCLC shelf. Preserve actual retrieval traces and the final answer.
6. Check the owner-agreed scientific/evidence rubric and link sources. End
   with Needs owner review if any required human check remains unfinished.

No automatic retry, cloud deployment, scientific data refresh or live-web
research under this profile. Model-use/indexing limits need an approved scope;
the draft does not choose them from an assumed runtime estimate.
